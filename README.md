## ✦ What is NexTime?

University life is scattered across timetables, attendance portals, WhatsApp groups, assignment reminders, notes, productivity apps and random PDFs.

**NexTime brings it together.**

NexTime is designed as a **Student Operating System** — a single academic command center where your timetable becomes the core context that powers the rest of your student life.

Instead of asking:

> "Where was my class again?"

> "How much attendance do I have?"

> "What assignment is due?"

> "When do I actually have time to study?"

> "Is my friend in class?"

NexTime brings all of that into one place.
